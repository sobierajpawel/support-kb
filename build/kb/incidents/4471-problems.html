The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants (all eleven on file) type 07/04 for 7 April and get 4 July. This is the problem #4471 describes, and the fix shipped on 5 October.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants on booking-v3 type what the hint asks, month first, and the form reads it day first. The symptom is the same flipped date. The cause is the hint, so the parser fix does not touch it. It comes only through the form channel of booking-v3 tenants.

one-day: the date is right, but the event lands a day early. No day and month are swapped. One client sees Tuesday, another sees Monday for the same meeting; an invite arrives “before” the booking. That points at a time zone conversion, not at date parsing. The desk already read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), but the incident page still counts them under parser.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Merging is by subject line (decision 0001). All three problems produce the same subjects: “wrong day”, “date is wrong”.
  • Intake tags each report from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale, form and channel, so the tags disagree with the cause (see the tag column on the #4471 page).
  • The #4471 table counts by cause and leaves the one-day moves inside parser as a footnote.

What to do

  1. Keep one ticket per problem. #3 stays open until the one-day reports stop.
  2. Close a ticket when engineering resolves its own problem, not when the merged ticket closes.
  3. Split by tenant, form and channel, not by subject, when a big ticket grows.
